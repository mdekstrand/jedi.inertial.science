# JEDI SEIZE
<center style="font-size:20px"><i>Seize the Means of Retrieval</i></center>
<br/><br/>

Today, a handful of large for-profit technology corporations exercise disproportionate power and control over our global information ecosystems.
This concentration of power, in the backdrop of the technology industry's emerging technofascist projects<sup><a href="https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/The-Nerd-Reich/Gil-Duran/9781668221402">1</a><a href="https://www.theguardian.com/technology/ng-interactive/2025/jan/29/silicon-valley-rightwing-technofascism">2</a></sup>, raises serious concerns of capture of our information platforms and their weaponization for mass surveillance and manipulation of public opinion.

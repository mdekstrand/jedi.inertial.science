# JEDI SEIZE
<center><i style="font-size:20px">Seize the Means of Retrieval</i></center>
<br/><br/>

Today, a handful of large for-profit technology corporations exercise disproportionate power and control over our global information ecosystems.
This concentration of power, in the backdrop of the technology industry's emerging technofascist projects<sup><a href="https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/The-Nerd-Reich/Gil-Duran/9781668221402">1</a></sup>


growing alignment with authoritarianism~\citep{becca2025headed, lafrance2024rise, klein2025rise, down2026palantir, kang2024silicon, davies2026ice, chayka2025techno} and compilicity in transnational repression~\citep{wang2020wechat}---raises serious concerns of capture of our information platforms and their weaponization for mass surveillance and manipulation of public opinion.
